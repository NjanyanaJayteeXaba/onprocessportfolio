Njanyana Xaba Also Known As "Jay-Tee Xaba"

Hello there! I'm JayTee, an IT professional and a passionate second-year student in the Diploma of Information and Communication Technology at Cape Peninsula University of Technology.

During my last year, I developed a solid foundation in programming through my studies in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. These languages not only gave me the skills for web development but also sparked my love for building things that matter.

Besides my coding abilities, I've recently started delving into Java, a language widely used across applications. This new journey has introduced me to object-oriented programming, pushing my problem-solving skills further.

In addition to my academic interests, I have a passion for exploring the digital realm. I love discovering emerging technologies and trends that could shape our future. Being part of the tech world means embracing change and always seeking growth.

My Goals as an IT Professional:

  • Seek Employment as a Developer: Pursue job opportunities that allow me to apply my skills and contribute to innovative solutions in the tech industry.
  • Develop a Strong Technical Foundation: Build expertise in core programming languages such as Java, Python, and JavaScript to enhance my coding skills and open up opportunities for advanced projects.
  • Engage in Real-World Projects: Seek internships and collaborative projects that allow me to apply my knowledge of web development, networks, and software solutions in practical settings.
  • Expand My Professional Network: Actively participate in tech meetups, online forums, and conferences to connect with industry professionals, share ideas, and learn about the latest developments in the field.
  • Pursue Continuous Learning: Stay updated with emerging technologies and trends in the IT field by taking online courses, attending workshops, and obtaining relevant certifications.
  • Contribute to Meaningful Projects: Aim to work on projects that make a positive impact in my community or industry, focusing on developing solutions that address real-world challenges.

What Drives Me: Art, Ambition & Authenticity

I love art. I am driven by a deep desire to build something great for people like me—people who don’t always have access or opportunity, who might not have the right tools, but still want to create and belong.
