The Story

I'm Njanyana
"JayTee" Xaba

I grew up in a small town in the Free State with limited access to technology. Moving to Cape Town to study ICT at CPUT changed everything — it opened my eyes to a world of opportunity and showed me the gap between raw potential and real-world exposure. That gap is exactly what fuels me.

While my Higher Certificate and first two years gave me a foundation in HTML, CSS, and Java, my real education happened outside the classroom. I taught myself to deploy sites, manage domains, and solve problems you won't find in any textbook. That sparked something: a love for building things that matter — not just for a grade, but for a purpose.
