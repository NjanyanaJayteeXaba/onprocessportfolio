I'mpilo (Life) – A Song from Nothing

This is what happens when the hunger to create is stronger than the lack of resources. Made with zero budget, this track was born from pure will. The beat? My phone. The vocals? Recorded on a basic Android app by my brother, **Xaba Moeketsi "Notation"**, and my friend, **Mahlangu Maxwell "Thee Ninety-Nine"**. The song is called "I'mpilo"—Life—and it’s about our struggle, our resilience. It’s a lesson: the most powerful tool you have is your own fire.

Made with: Canva & a SingPlay Android App
